Get involved

Three ways in.

The program has grown faster than the handful of people running it. If the idea matters to you and you will give it real time, there is a place for you.

The 2026 mentee cohort is full. Applications for the 2027 intake will be posted here when they open. Mentor applications stay open all year — we are always looking for researchers to join the pool, and you can apply at any time.

For students

Apply as a mentee

You are an undergraduate or master’s student at a Bangladeshi university, curious about research, and willing to put in the hours. No prior experience needed — tell us what interests you and we will try to match you with a mentor who works on it.

Applications closed for 2026. The 2027 intake will be announced here.

For researchers

Apply as a mentor

You are a Bangladeshi PhD student, postdoc, faculty member or industry researcher who can give one or two hours a week. There is no deadline for this one — we take mentor applications year-round and match new mentors into the next cycle. You will work with one to three students, and join a community of mentors who help each other with their own research too.

For students, right now

Help us run it

Undergraduates are welcome on the operations side — outreach, scheduling, keeping records straight, and the administrative work that keeps a cycle moving. It is a real way into the community while the mentee call is closed, and you learn how a program like this actually works.

Reach out on Facebook

Questions about the program, or an institution interested in working with us? Send it through our contact form, or message us on Facebook — whichever is easier.
