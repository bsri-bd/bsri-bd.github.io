Mentors

Twenty-four researchers, indexed by what they actually work on.

Mentees are matched to a mentor whose work overlaps their interests — so the range below is the point, not the headcount.

Computing, AI & security

Prasun Datta

PhD student · Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute

Works on the mathematical foundations of deep learning — how networks learn representations through gradient-based optimisation, why some learned features generalise, and how adversarial training reshapes hidden layers. The aim is rigorous theory for why deep networks work, when they fail, and how to make them reliable.

  • Theoretical deep learning
  • Optimization theory
  • Representation learning
  • Adversarial robustness

WebsiteScholar

Imranur Rahman

PhD student · North Carolina State University

Studies software security, particularly the attack surface created by vulnerable dependencies in the software supply chain. His work measures the costs and benefits of different dependency declaration methods as a way of reducing exposure to n-day attacks.

  • Software supply chain security
  • Vulnerable dependencies
  • Empirical software engineering

WebsiteScholar

Sk Ruhul Azgor

PhD student · Pennsylvania State University

Works in theoretical computer science, on approximation algorithms, graph theory, linear graph layouts and dynamic algorithms.

  • Approximation algorithms
  • Graph theory
  • Linear graph layout
  • Dynamic algorithms

Rasman Mubtasim Swargo

Master’s student · Missouri University of Science and Technology

Applies machine learning to systems, focusing on high-performance data transfer across wide-area research networks, with adaptive controllers that replace brittle hand-tuned heuristics with reinforcement learning — tested on research testbeds such as FABRIC and CloudLab.

  • ML for systems
  • Reinforcement learning
  • High-performance data transfer
  • Network optimization

WebsiteScholar

Md Kamruzzaman Kamrul

PhD student · Purdue University

Applies machine learning and natural language processing across finance and civil engineering, including large language models for time-series forecasting and deepfake detection, alongside work on the seismic response of soil and embankments.

  • Machine learning
  • Large language models
  • Natural language processing
  • Civil engineering

Physics & astronomy

Pranta Saha

PhD student · University of California, Davis

Integrates solid-state defects — excellent spin qubits — with high-index nanophotonic devices, building hardware for on-chip quantum information processing.

  • Quantum nanophotonics
  • Spin qubits
  • On-chip photonics
  • Electromagnetic modelling

Scholar

Moonzarin Reza

PhD student · Texas A&M University

Works at the intersection of machine learning and cosmology, using simulation-based inference to recover cosmological parameters from Type Ia supernova light curves.

  • Cosmology
  • Simulation-based inference
  • Supernova light curves
  • Machine learning

WebsiteScholar

Syeda Lammim Ahad

Postdoctoral fellow · Waterloo Centre for Astrophysics

Studies how galaxies form and evolve across cosmic time in different environments, using cosmological hydrodynamic simulations and photometric surveys to analyse large samples of galaxies and clusters — and to ask when, how and why galaxies stop forming stars.

  • Extragalactic astronomy
  • Galaxy evolution
  • Cosmological simulations
  • Big data analysis

Scholar

Tabassum S Tanvir

Postdoctoral researcher · Iowa State University

Studies star and planet formation: how turbulence and initial mass function variations shape stellar birth, and how non-ideal magnetohydrodynamic effects govern protoplanetary disc evolution and the growth of planetary embryos.

  • Theoretical astrophysics
  • Star formation
  • Protoplanetary discs
  • Magnetohydrodynamics

Scholar

Probes the physics of low-dimensional materials to build multifunctional opto-electro-mechanical nanodevices, with the goal of unconventional memory, logic and energy-harvesting devices that cut the energy cost of computation.

  • Scanning probe microscopy

Faculty member · Bangladesh Army University of Engineering & Technology

Works on computational fluid dynamics and heat transfer, including conjugate mixed convection and solar air heaters, alongside density functional theory and bio-inspired aerofoil aerodynamics.

  • Heat transfer

Arafat Anzir

PhD student · Texas State University

Works where bio-inspired materials meet computational mechanics and deep learning, analysing CT scan data of pangolin scales to understand how natural armour achieves damage tolerance, and applying physics-informed neural networks to biomechanical prediction.

  • Biomimetics
  • Biomechanics
  • Solid mechanics
  • Physics-informed neural networks

  • Data-driven assessment

Scholar

Rahul Sarker

University of Calgary

Uses low-value biomass — specifically aspen fibre — in fused filament fabrication to make wood-PLA biocomposites, working on the chemical treatments that let hydrophilic wood fibre bond with a hydrophobic polymer matrix, and on how much fibre a print can carry before it clogs.

  • Natural fibre composites
  • 3D printing
  • Life-cycle assessment
  • Technoeconomic analysis
