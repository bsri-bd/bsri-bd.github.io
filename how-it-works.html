How it works

What we set out to do for every mentee.

Four commitments shape how mentors work with the students matched to them.

Research skills and knowledge

Equip mentees with advanced research methodologies, critical analysis skills, and comprehensive knowledge in their respective fields.

Networking and collaboration

Create a supportive community where mentees can connect with peers, mentors, and industry professionals to expand their professional networks.

Career readiness

Equip mentees with the essential tools and support to successfully transition into their chosen careers, ensuring they are well-prepared for the job market and professional environments.

Personal growth and academic success

Support the holistic development of mentees, enhancing their confidence, time management, and problem-solving abilities to achieve academic excellence.

In practice a mentee meets their mentor weekly or fortnightly over video, works on a project scoped to their level, and gets help with the things that are hard to learn alone — reading the literature, framing a question, writing it up. Some pairs aim at a conference paper or publication; others aim at skills, with no formal output. That is agreed between mentor and mentee at the start.
