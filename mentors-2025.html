2025 cohort

The seventeen who went first.

These researchers mentored the first BSRI cohort, between early 2025 and the close of that cycle. Eight of them are mentoring again in 2026.

Computing, AI & interaction

Md Kamruzzaman Kamrul

PhD student · Purdue University

Also mentoring in 2026

Applies machine learning and natural language processing across finance and civil engineering, including large language models for time-series forecasting and deepfake detection, alongside work on the seismic response of soil and embankments.

  • Machine learning
  • Large language models
  • Natural language processing
  • Civil engineering

Scholar

Ibrahim Tahmid

Studies how much of a person’s thinking can be read from their gaze, and uses eye tracking to build real-time assistants for immersive environments — along with the design problems such agents raise.

  • Augmented & virtual reality
  • Human–computer interaction
  • Eye tracking
  • Human-centered AI

WebsiteScholar

Hasan Mahmud Prottoy

Investigates how people adapt technology to work around infrastructural limits, particularly in the global south, drawing on critical HCI, postcolonial computing and participatory design to make technology design more equitable.

  • Human–computer interaction
  • CSCW
  • Social computing
  • ICT for development

WebsiteScholar

Mostofa Rafid Uddin

Builds unsupervised and self-supervised methods for multi-scale generative modelling of subcellular objects, and adapts them to 2D and 3D biological images.

  • Machine learning
  • Computational biology
  • Computer vision
  • Generative modelling

Scholar

Sourav Saha

Applies pretrained language models — transformers and state space models — to genomics data, particularly post-transcriptional regulatory mechanisms, with earlier research and industry work in Bangla NLP.

  • Computational biology
  • Large language models
  • Natural language processing
  • Bangla NLP

Scholar

Kazi Mejbaul Islam

Explores fault attacks on deep neural network accelerators across FPGA, TPU and GPU hardware.

  • Hardware security
  • Fault attacks
  • FPGA
  • ML accelerator design

Scholar

Computation in biology & health

Abdul Muntakim Rafi

PhD student · University of British Columbia

Also mentoring in 2026

Works on machine learning for regulatory genomics — predicting how DNA sequence determines gene expression, and what that reveals about the non-coding genome.

  • Regulatory genomics
  • Computational biology
  • Applied machine learning

Scholar

Tasnim Nishat Islam

PhD student · University of Maryland, Baltimore County

Also mentoring in 2026

Develops machine learning for biomedical signals on wearable devices, including real-time stress detection from ECG and PPG data as a scalable alternative to clinic-based mental health assessment.

  • Wearable devices
  • Biomedical signals
  • Lightweight machine learning
  • Digital health

Scholar

Physics & astronomy

Syeda Lammim Ahad

Postdoctoral fellow · Waterloo Centre for Astrophysics

Also mentoring in 2026

Studies how galaxies form and evolve across cosmic time in different environments, using cosmological hydrodynamic simulations and photometric surveys to analyse large samples of galaxies and clusters — and to ask when, how and why galaxies stop forming stars.

  • Extragalactic astronomy
  • Galaxy evolution
  • Cosmological simulations
  • Big data analysis

Scholar

Tabassum Shahriar Tanvir

Postdoctoral researcher · Iowa State University

Also mentoring in 2026

Studies star and planet formation: how turbulence and initial mass function variations shape stellar birth, and how non-ideal magnetohydrodynamic effects govern protoplanetary disc evolution and the growth of planetary embryos.

  • Theoretical astrophysics
  • Star formation
  • Protoplanetary discs
  • Magnetohydrodynamics

Scholar

Md Minaruzzaman

PhD student · The Ohio State University

Also mentoring in 2026

Studies how nuclear materials behave under irradiation, combining theoretical and computational modelling with hands-on experiment so that simulations can be validated against real observations.

  • Nuclear materials
  • Irradiation damage
  • Condensed matter physics
  • Computational modelling

WebsiteScholar

Materials & nanotechnology

Shahriar Nahid

Postdoctoral researcher · Stanford University

Also mentoring in 2026

Probes the physics of low-dimensional materials to build multifunctional opto-electro-mechanical nanodevices, with the goal of unconventional memory, logic and energy-harvesting devices that cut the energy cost of computation.

  • Two-dimensional materials
  • Quantum materials
  • Ferroelectricity
  • Scanning probe microscopy

WebsiteScholar

Turash Haque Pial

Models bio and soft materials and nano-microfluidic systems, combining physical chemistry, statistical physics and machine learning. Projects include polyelectrolyte-grafted nanochannels, peptide-amphiphile nanofibre formation, and siRNA payload distribution in lipid nanoparticles.

  • Computational materials science
  • Soft matter physics
  • Microfluidics
  • Self-assembly

Scholar

Sadman Sadeed Omee

PhD candidate · University of South Carolina

Develops graph neural networks, transformers and diffusion models for materials informatics — crystal structure prediction, property prediction, and generative modelling of materials. The crystal structure problem is a close cousin of protein structure prediction.

  • Graph neural networks
  • Diffusion models
  • Computational materials science
  • AI for science

WebsiteScholar

Mechanical, energy & transport

Rahul Sarker

University of Calgary

Also mentoring in 2026

Uses low-value biomass — specifically aspen fibre — in fused filament fabrication to make wood-PLA biocomposites, working on the chemical treatments that let hydrophilic wood fibre bond with a hydrophobic polymer matrix, and on how much fibre a print can carry before it clogs.

  • Natural fibre composites
  • 3D printing
  • Life-cycle assessment
  • Technoeconomic analysis

Scholar

Rahat Mahmud Khan

Works on defrosting and deicing strategies that cut energy use and downtime, studying how frost nucleates and grows across temperature, humidity and surface wettability, and developing electro-thermal defrosting using conductive thin-film coatings.

  • Thermal management
  • Heat transfer
  • Frost nucleation
  • Fire dynamics

Scholar

Ahmed Imtiaz Zamee

Works on transportation safety, currently wrong-way driving detection technologies and school bus safety, using statistical analysis, machine learning and video analytics alongside survey data collection.

  • Transportation safety
  • Autonomous vehicles
  • Video analytics
  • Transportation policy
