Code of conduct

The terms everyone agrees to.

This code applies to all participants — mentors, mentees, organizers, and guests — in any BSRI-affiliated project or activity.

Respect

Treat everyone involved with respect and professionalism.

Equity, diversity, inclusivity

Promote equal opportunities, value diversity, and strive for inclusivity.

A harassment-free environment

No form of harassment, bullying, or discrimination will be tolerated.

Confidentiality

Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information shared within collaborations.

Scientific and academic integrity

Uphold honesty, objectivity, and transparency in research and academic pursuits. Plagiarism, fabrication or falsification of data, and misrepresentation of credentials are prohibited.

Reporting. Violations may be reported using the form below, and may be reported anonymously. Consequences range from a warning through to permanent removal from the programme and notification of the individual’s institution. Retaliation against anyone who reports a violation is itself a violation.

This code is reviewed annually and revised as necessary.
